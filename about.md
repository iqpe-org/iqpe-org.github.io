The Institute for Quantitative Political Economy is a new kind of think tank: a consortium of expertise dedicated to combining sophisticated analysis with dialectical and historical materialism. Through the science of political economy, we strive to empower leaders and ordinary people alike to build democratic social and economic structures. A better world is possible, and it is up to us to build it.
